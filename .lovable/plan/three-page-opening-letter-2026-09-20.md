# Three-page opening letter

## What will change
- Replace the immediate full-page message with a closed, animated envelope as page 1.
- Opening the envelope reveals page 2: the sincere apology and a clear “Continue” action.
- Page 3 reveals the final promise and forgiveness question, with a gentle closing animation.
- Keep the existing floral photograph, romantic styling, falling petals, and mobile-friendly layout.

## Interaction
- Each page advances only when the recipient taps the letter control.
- Show a subtle 1 of 3, 2 of 3, and 3 of 3 progress marker.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and keep controls keyboard accessible.

## Technical details
- Use one focused React state flow for the three stages.
- Reuse the existing design tokens and button component.
- Verify the complete sequence at desktop and mobile sizes.
